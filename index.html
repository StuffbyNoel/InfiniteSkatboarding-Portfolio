THE CONCEPT

Infinite Skateboarding is a seamless 3D looping animation built around rhythm, momentum, and continuous camera motion. Every frame was timed so the environment, board physics, and lighting cycle back effortlessly without a visible cut.

The project combines stylized environment modeling, dynamic wheel and body animation, and atmospheric lighting passes to create a hypnotic, endlessly replayable visual loop.
